# severrir

## What this is
A commission portfolio for **severrir**, a Roblox/Luau systems scripter and full-stack developer who sells built-to-order game systems.

## Who it is for
Technically literate buyers: Roblox studio owners, game leads, and other developers who need a system built and can read code. They are evaluating architecture quality and reliability, not visual flair.

## The job of the site
Prove the work is well-engineered, then convert to a booking request. Every section either supplies evidence (showcase, services) or removes friction from commissioning (pricing, FAQ, booking).

## Mode
**Experience** on `/` (the work leads), **Persuade** on pricing and `/booking`.

## Product truth
- Commissions are open. Minimum order **$25**.
- Four tiers: per-job ($25–50), per-system ($75–150), scoped project ($200–500+), custom retainer.
- Payment: PayPal (primary), Bitcoin, Ethereum, EU bank transfer, Robux.
- Delivery: order request → work completed and showcased → payment confirmed → files delivered. Or direct collaboration inside the client's Studio.
- Upfront payment for smaller projects; milestone-based otherwise.
- Inbound goes through Formspree (`xgojkepa`), which pings severrir on Discord.
- Booking requires a Discord username; email is optional.

## Constraints
- Next.js App Router, TypeScript strict, Tailwind v4, Framer Motion, Howler, lucide-react.
- Visual world is pinned in CLAUDE.md: navy `#000814` base, single gold accent `#FFC300`, Geist Sans/Mono. No second accent hue.
- Anti-slop rules in CLAUDE.md are binding: no emoji, no neon glow, no rainbow gradients, no bouncy motion, no eyebrow labels, no trailing arrows.
